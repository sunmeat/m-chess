# Chess piece artwork credits

M-Chess offers 99 visual choices made from five upstream piece sets and CSS color treatments. A color treatment is a derivative visual variation, not a separate original artwork set.

## Included sources

- **RhosGFX** — CC0 1.0. Source: https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/rhosgfx . CC0 is intended to dedicate the work to the public domain; retain this record as provenance.
- **Fantasy** — MIT. Artwork by Maurizio Monge. Source: https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art/tree/main/fantasy . Preserve the upstream copyright and permission notice when redistributing the artwork.
- **Spatial** — MIT. Artwork by Maurizio Monge. Source: https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art/tree/main/spatial . Preserve the upstream copyright and permission notice when redistributing the artwork.
- **Celtic** — MIT. Artwork by Maurizio Monge. Source: https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art/tree/main/celtic . Preserve the upstream copyright and permission notice when redistributing the artwork.
- **Chessnut** — Apache License 2.0. Artwork by Alexis Luengas. Source: https://github.com/LexLuengas/chessnut-pieces . Follow Apache-2.0 notice and attribution requirements when redistributing the artwork.

The SVG assets are loaded from the Lichess repository at runtime. Upstream artwork remains subject to its respective license.
